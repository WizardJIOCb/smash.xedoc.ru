# Visual brief — 2026-11-13

Status: locked 2026-11-13. User accepted the three original v1 concepts: «Берём героя и мир (рекомендую)». Rigging and runtime visual verification remain ahead.

- Evidence: 13 likes, 6 dislikes, no selected direction and no comments. All six forest-server images liked; F.I.S.T. 3/3 and courtyard liked; Rayman 2/2 liked. Both Mark of the Ninja images, constructivist poster, two saturated Guacamelee compositions disliked.
- Inference: painterly depth, soft atmospheric light, tactile materials and expressive rounded characters appeal more than flat graphic silhouettes or a poster aesthetic. This is a hypothesis to show in actual original concepts.
- Direction: a lived-in industrial courtyard being reclaimed by roots, moss and luminous growth. Large soft shapes and mist create depth; warm windows keep the city human.
- Hero: original anthropomorphic moose in a worn burgundy tracksuit with cream stripes, dark trousers, pale sneakers; broad snout, paddle antlers, heavy shoulders and amused confidence. No existing character copied.
- Palette proposal: #132630 shadow, #386264 atmospheric teal, #718068 moss, #e2b36e warm windows, #e7d3ac hero highlights; #e44a42 reserved for Firewall hazards.
- Shape: asymmetrical organic arches wrapping rectangular panel buildings and server cabinets; clear horizontal playable ledges; hero and enemies separate from background.
- Techniques: 4+ depth planes; warm/cool light separation; spores/rain caught in light; moss and cables overlap industrial surfaces; district-wide quarantine light transformation.
- Avoid: flat black-on-black figures, constructivist poster layout, simultaneous neon accents everywhere, pixel art, 3D plastic look, busy HUD.
- Generation outputs are visual targets, not gameplay captures, rigged assets, or evidence of final browser performance.
